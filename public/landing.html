Features

Everything your study group needs

Built for students who actually want to understand the material — not just memorize it.

🤖
AI that knows your notes
Ask Claude anything about your notebook. It reads all your uploaded notes and answers in context — like a tutor who did all the reading.
👥
Collaborative by default
Invite classmates to a notebook with a link. Everyone can upload notes, and the whole group can query the AI together.
📄
Upload anything
Drop in PDFs, typed notes, screenshots — whatever you use to study. scholr indexes it all for AI search.
⚡
Instant answers
No more waiting for your study group to text back. Claude answers in seconds with citations from your own notes.
🔒
Private and secure
Your notes stay in your notebook. Share only with people you invite — never public by default.
🎨
Actually beautiful
A workspace dark enough for late-night study sessions. Purple accents, clean typography, zero clutter.
How it works

Up and running in minutes

No complicated setup. Just sign up and start studying smarter.

01
Create a notebook
Make one notebook per class or topic. Give it a name and subject — scholr uses that to give the AI context.
02
Add your notes
Upload PDFs, paste in text, or type directly. Your whole study group can contribute — the more notes, the smarter the AI.
03
Ask Claude anything
Open the notebook and start chatting. "Explain the French Revolution", "What's on the Unit 3 test?", "Quiz me on Chapter 5."
04
Ace the test
Walk into the exam actually knowing the material. scholr's not a shortcut — it's the fastest path to actually understanding.
